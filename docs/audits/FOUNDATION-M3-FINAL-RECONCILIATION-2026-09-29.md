# FOUNDATION-M3 — Final Reconciliation Audit

**Date:** 2026-09-29  
**Status:** COMPLETE — REQUIRED MASTER-PLAN RECONCILIATION APPLIED  
**Scope:** Re-audit the Main Product Master Plan against the new SOLO/SIA discoveries, locked M2 Foundation, 2026 competitive baseline and current multi-agent research.  
**Environment name:** SOLO is temporary.  
**Intelligent operator:** SIA.

---

## 1. Executive verdict

**Verdict: the Foundation remains strong, but the original M3 commercial/product-family model is superseded.**

The new discoveries do **not** invalidate the majority of M2.

They **do** invalidate the central M3 assumption that the customer experience should be organized primarily as a family of separately operated specialist products.

The stronger architecture is:

> **One company → one operating environment → one SIA company brain → many users/roles/lenses → many governed capabilities → dynamic specialists/workflows behind SIA.**

The old specialist-product model becomes historical discovery evidence.

---

## 2. What changed

Original M3 assumed:

```
Family
├── Marketing product
├── Support product
├── future specialist products
└── Jarvis / company intelligence above them
```

Revised model:

```
Company
        ↓
Unified Operating Environment
        ↓
Company Graph + Context + Artifacts
        ↓
SIA — Company Brain / User-Facing Intelligence
        ↓
Role / Responsibility / Domain Lenses
        ↓
Deterministic Workflows + Specialist Workers + Human Work
        ↓
Providers / Systems / Authoritative Domain Sources
```

The primary abstraction is now **the company operating model**, not the product catalog.

### PASS — retain

The following M2 architecture remains directly useful:

- data-governance contract;
- contract/version/migration model;
- platform-operator JIT authority;
- coarse-grained runtime architecture;
- workload identity;
- operational lifecycle separation.

These are more valuable under the new SIA model because one company brain requires strong deterministic governance.

### REINTERPRET — no architectural reset

#### Product dimension
The M2 product dimension remains useful as an **internal authority/domain/capability boundary**, but no longer implies a separate customer-facing application or commercial product.

#### Atomic SKUs
Atomic entitlement mechanics remain useful.

The entitled object may now be:
- role/capability pack;
- advanced domain capability;
- specialist-worker capacity;
- connector/provider capability;
- high-cost AI feature;
- premium governance/enterprise capability.

It does not have to be a separate specialist application.

#### Resource Links
Remain valid for:
- artifacts;
- reports;
- people;
- processes;
- tasks;
- meetings;
- domain records;
- admin/configuration;
- external authoritative resources.

They are no longer primarily “cross-product” links.

#### Domain runtimes
Domain-owned runtimes remain allowed where semantics/security/scale justify them.

They do not automatically become separate commercial products.

# 6. New canonical product decisions

## R1 — One Company / One Operating Environment

One tenant/company has one coherent operating environment.

Departments and roles are scopes/lenses, not separate installations.

---

## R2 — Role / Responsibility Operating Model

Model:
- departments;
- roles;
- responsibilities;
- processes;
- systems;
- metrics;
- artifacts;
- relationships;
- actions.

Role title is only a starting archetype.

Actual responsibilities customize the lens.

---

## R3 — Three Onboarding Modes

Support:

1. **Adapt to Us**
2. **Help Us Build**
3. **Start with Standard**

All use the same underlying operating-model engine.

---

## R4 — Reference / Configured / Observed Process

For important processes preserve:

- reference process;
- configured company process;
- observed real process.

SIA can detect:
- process drift;
- missing ownership;
- bottlenecks;
- bypassed approvals;
- training/adoption problems;
- automation opportunities.

---

## R5 — SIA Company Brain

SIA is the single visible intelligent operator.

It may:
- answer;
- analyze;
- recommend;
- plan;
- coordinate;
- delegate;
- execute;
- supervise;
- report;
- temporarily provide digital capacity.

Principle:

> **No artificial functional ceiling; bounded authority always.**

---

## R6 — SIA Supervisor + Dynamic Specialists

Do not build:
- one giant prompt/tool agent;
- one permanent agent per role.

Build:
- SIA Supervisor;
- Specialist Registry;
- temporary/run-scoped specialists;
- deterministic workflows;
- shared context/artifacts.

Principle:

> **One brain. Many specialist capabilities. Spawn workers when the work justifies them.**

---

## R7 — Role Is Not Agent

Company Graph roles model work and authority.

Specialists model reusable reasoning/execution capability.

One role may use:
- SIA directly;
- multiple specialists;
- deterministic automation;
- humans;
- hybrid execution.

---

## R8 — Prebuilt Interactive Runtime

SIA does not generate arbitrary production UI code.

Build:
- approved component registry;
- typed surface protocol;
- tested workspace patterns;
- domain components;
- artifact renderers/editors.

SIA composes and populates approved software.

---

## R9 — Persistent Artifacts

Reports, process maps, plans, meetings, analyses and decisions are durable versioned objects.

Agent results should reference artifacts instead of passing giant text blobs among agents.

---

## R10 — Company Graph

Maintain permission/provenance-aware relationships among:
- people;
- departments;
- roles;
- responsibilities;
- systems;
- processes;
- metrics;
- artifacts;
- actions;
- meetings;
- resources.

Truth states include:
- authoritative;
- verified;
- observed;
- inferred;
- claimed;
- conflicting.

---

## R11 — Digital Workforce / Hybrid Work

A responsibility can be fulfilled by:
- human;
- deterministic automation;
- SIA Specialist;
- hybrid.

SIA may detect missing/overloaded capacity and propose options.

Digital workers follow:
**Draft → Simulation → Shadow → Supervised → Bounded Autonomous → Suspended/Retired**

No self-promotion.

---

## R12 — Deterministic Workflow Spine

Repeatable sequential work uses deterministic durable workflows.

AI is inserted where:
- ambiguity;
- judgment;
- synthesis;
- classification;
- interpretation

actually benefit from AI.

---

SIA chooses among:
- DIRECT;
- WORKFLOW;

- HYBRID.

Routing considers:
- quality;

- risk;

# 8. Cost / trade-off audit

## Cost increases

### Operating-model library
High editorial/product investment.

Need to research, define, version and evaluate:
- roles;
- responsibilities;
- workflows;
- metrics;
- artifacts;
- domain semantics.

### Component/workspace library
High upfront UX/engineering investment.

Benefit:
- lower runtime generation risk;
- faster interfaces;
- consistent quality;
- reuse.

### Company Graph / indexing
Meaningful storage/indexing/reconciliation complexity.

Need:
- provenance;
- freshness;
- conflict handling;
- permission filtering.

### Specialist evaluation
Every specialist requires:
- task contract;
- tools;
- evals;
- budgets;
- failure handling.

### Meeting/media
Potentially high provider, transcription and storage cost.

### Free tier
Direct acquisition cost must be capped and measured.

## Cost decreases

### Fewer product codebases
Major reduction in:
- duplicated navigation;
- duplicate setup;
- duplicate role systems;
- duplicated connectors;
- separate dashboards;
- duplicated AI assistants.

### Dynamic specialists
Lower cost than permanent one-agent-per-role architecture.

### Deterministic workflows
Lower AI usage and higher repeatability for known work.

### Pre-indexed history
Lower repeated token/context cost.

### Shared artifacts/components
Lower repeated generation and implementation cost.

### One company model
Reduces synchronization/integration overhead between internal “products.”

# 12. Final audit verdict

**PASS AFTER RECONCILIATION.**

The new thesis is compatible with the strongest parts of M1/M2 and materially simplifies the old multi-product customer experience.

The main price is substantial:
- operating-model content;
- graph/index infrastructure;
- component/workspace library;
- specialist evaluation;
- governance;
- onboarding intelligence.

The main payoff is also substantial:
- one product architecture;
- stronger reuse;
- lower duplication;
- lower AI waste;
- clearer company context;
- role-specific GTM without role-specific products;
- coherent solo-to-enterprise growth;
- stronger SIA experience.

This revised plan should replace the old M3 product-family master plan as current authority.
