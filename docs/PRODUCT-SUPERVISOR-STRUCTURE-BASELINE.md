# Admonk Product Supervisor — Structure Baseline

**Baseline label:** v1.0.0-structure  
**Captured:** 2026-09-26  
**Current stable Product Supervisor release at capture:** v1.1.0  
**Purpose:** Freeze the current structural/governance shape before researching and authoring Admonk's foundational product-development doctrine.

## What this baseline freezes

The baseline preserves the current reusable system shape:
- Product Supervisor lifecycle and gates;
- project-governance artifacts;
- specialist skill routing;
- organization/reuse discipline;
- simplicity-first engineering discipline;
- complexity budget;
- release audit;
- evidence/exception/control artifacts;
- model-neutral supervisor architecture.

## What this baseline does NOT freeze

It does not declare the current principles, standards, thresholds, or defaults to be fully researched doctrine.

In particular, the following still require deliberate research and human approval:
- what "premium" means;
- what simplicity means;
- what quality standards are non-negotiable;
- when AI may act autonomously;
- what evidence is sufficient at each gate;
- how speed/cost/quality/scope trade-offs are made;
- what architecture/scaling defaults Admonk should adopt;
- what design, accessibility, engineering, security, AI and operations standards should become durable studio doctrine.

## Rule

> **The repository structure is reusable. The doctrine must be earned.**

This baseline is the comparison point for the doctrine-research phase. Do not silently rewrite the baseline to make later conclusions appear pre-existing.
