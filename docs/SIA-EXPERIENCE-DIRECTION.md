**Status:** CURRENT CANONICAL EXPERIENCE DIRECTION

**Environment name:** SOLO is temporary.

---

## 1. Identity

SIA is the single visible intelligent operator/company brain inside the unified company operating environment.

SIA is not:
- the source database;
- the permission system;
- the deterministic workflow engine;
- every specialist worker;
- a chat-only assistant.

SIA coordinates those systems.

---

## 2. Experience promise

> **Ask SIA. See the work. Interact with it. Act without losing context.**

SIA should:
- acknowledge immediately;
- show truthful progress;
- open the right surface;
- preserve the current context;
- explain what is happening;
- use specialists/workflows invisibly where appropriate;
- return verifiable results.

---

## 3. Interaction model

### Conversation / voice
Questions, commands, clarification.

### Inline components
Metrics, small charts, approvals, statuses.

### Context panel
Contacts, meeting setup, filters, quick actions, resource details.

### Dynamic workspace
Reports, investigations, process maps, scenario planning, meetings.

### Durable structured workspace
Dense/bulk/admin work when persistent precision is superior.

---

## 4. SIA Surface Runtime

SIA composes from an approved component registry.

Examples:
- Metric;
- Chart;
- Table;
- ProcessMap;
- RelationshipGraph;
- EvidencePanel;
- Finding;
- Recommendation;
- Plan;
- Timeline;
- ContactPicker;
- MeetingPanel;
- Approval;
- TaskList;
- Comparison;
- DocumentViewer;
- Form;
- ArtifactView.

Every component has:
- schema;
- states;
- permissions;
- actions;
- accessibility;
- responsive behavior;
- typed events.

No arbitrary generated production UI code.

---

## 5. Shared interaction state

SIA and the client share typed context:

- active artifact;
- selected process node;
- selected chart point/range;
- selected people;
- current meeting draft;
- current approval;
- active role/domain lens;
- current resource.

This supports:
> “Why did this happen?”

after the user clicked a specific chart/process node.

---

## 6. SIA execution routing

SIA selects:

- DIRECT;
- WORKFLOW;
- SPECIALIST;
- TEAM;
- HYBRID.

The user normally sees SIA, not internal agent topology.

SIA may expose internal progress when useful:
- “Auditing 38 candidate files”
- “3 independent specialists are reviewing this”
- “Waiting for approval”

---

## 7. First-run experience

First onboarding should visibly and truthfully show:

**Connect → organize → discover → map → analyze → present → act**

SIA labels knowledge:
- authoritative;
- verified;
- observed;
- inferred;
- claimed;
- conflicting.

The onboarding is the first analysis, not administration before value.

---

## 8. Role-first adaptation

SIA begins from:
- department archetype;
- role archetype;
- responsibilities.

Then adapts to:
- company overrides;
- actual systems;
- observed process;
- permissions;
- individual scope.

---

## 9. Company brain behavior

SIA may:
- suggest;
- follow authorized orders;
- coordinate;
- delegate;
- instantiate temporary specialists;
- supervise;
- audit;
- report;
- escalate;
- hand work back to humans.

No artificial functional ceiling.

Authority remains bounded outside the model.

---

## 10. Core principle

> **SIA is one coherent intelligence. Specialists are execution capabilities, not separate personalities the user must manage.**
