# FOUNDATION-M3-05 — Standalone vs Connected Product Value

**Date:** 2026-09-28  
**Status:** LOCKED — OWNER SELECTED SCENARIO B  
**Milestone:** FOUNDATION-M3 — Main Product Master Plan  
**Method:** quick internal scan → two high-value sources → two scenarios → challenge → synthesis  
**Implementation authority:** None

## Internal scan

Locked direction:
- M3-01: specialist-first, platform-backed, progressively unified;
- M3-03: specialist products must provide meaningful standalone value;
- M3-04: product-scoped Jarvis is included; Company Intelligence is the cross-product add-on;
- specialist domains retain their own truth/workflows;
- connected value may expand through shared platform, context and Jarvis.

The remaining question:
> How much value should exist only when products are connected?

## External source 1 — Atlassian

Atlassian's 2024 Annual Report describes its System of Work as a **connected portfolio of products with discrete value propositions**, built on a common platform/data model. It explicitly treats individual product value and connected portfolio value as complementary rather than mutually exclusive.

Source:
https://investors.atlassian.com/files/doc_downloads/agm/2024/Oct/TEAM-2024-Annual-Report.pdf

Admonk implication:
- each product should have a clear value proposition on its own;
- platform connection should create additional value across teams, information and workflows;
- connected value should not require collapsing specialist products into one application.

## External source 2 — Microsoft

Microsoft's current Copilot/Work IQ guidance explains that richer intelligence comes from connecting signals across emails, files, chats, meetings, people, projects and connected apps. That broader context enables Copilot to surface risks, opportunities, follow-ups and next actions that isolated content cannot provide.

Source:
https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365-copilot/what-information-does-copilot-use-to-answer-my-prompt

Admonk implication:
- connected value should come from **new context and relationships**, not arbitrary feature withholding;
- cross-product intelligence can legitimately be more capable because it has more authorized evidence.

## Scenario A — Standalone parity; connection is mostly convenience

Each specialist product is fully complete and connecting products mainly adds:
- one login;
- shared navigation;
- shared settings;
- unified billing/admin;
- simple cross-links.

### Strengths
- simplest product promises;
- no customer feels pressured to expand;
- low cross-product coupling.

### Challenge
- weak reason to buy multiple products from Admonk;
- shared platform becomes mostly infrastructure;
- Jarvis Company Intelligence has limited differentiation;
- misses legitimate cross-domain insight/workflow value.

## Scenario B — Standalone complete; connected value compounds

Each specialist product fully delivers its **own promised domain outcome**.

Connecting products then unlocks genuinely new value that cannot exist inside one domain alone.

Examples:
- cross-domain priority brief;
- business issue traced across Marketing + Support evidence;
- company goals connected to departmental execution;
- shared contacts/entities resolved across domains where governance permits;
- cross-product workflow handoffs;
- executive visibility across products;
- Jarvis Company Intelligence reasoning over authorized multi-domain context.

### Strengths
- no artificial feature crippling;
- clear expansion value;
- matches the locked Company Intelligence add-on;
- gives platform/network effects without making one product dependent on another;
- customers can grow into Admonk rather than migrate into a completely different product.

### Challenge
- connected features require strong governance/provenance;
- cross-product semantics must be explicit;
- duplicated/conflicting entities need reconciliation;
- expansion value must be measurable rather than vague “synergy.”

## Synthesis

Choose **Scenario B** with a strict value rule.

### Standalone Value Floor

A specialist product is commercially valid alone only if it can:
- complete the core domain workflow;
- produce the promised domain outcome;
- operate with product-scoped Jarvis;
- support required domain analytics/evidence;
- administer its relevant users/settings/integrations through the shared platform;
- function without another specialist product being subscribed.

### Connected Value Test

A connected feature is justified when it creates value that **logically requires two or more domains**, such as:
1. cross-domain context;
2. cross-domain workflow;
3. company/executive aggregation;
4. entity/relationship intelligence across products;
5. coordination/decision support spanning products.

Do **not** put ordinary product features behind multi-product subscription simply to manufacture bundle value.

## Recommended lock

> **M3-05 — Complete Standalone Outcomes, Compounding Connected Value**
>
> Every Admonk specialist product must independently deliver its full promised domain outcome without requiring another specialist product.
>
> The shared platform may provide common identity, setup, integrations, governance and administration, but those shared dependencies do not make another commercial product mandatory.
>
> Connected products should unlock materially new value only where that value logically requires multiple authorized domains.
>
> Cross-product value should primarily come from richer context, relationships, coordination, workflows, executive visibility and Jarvis Company Intelligence—not from withholding ordinary specialist-product capabilities.
>
> A standalone customer should feel they bought a complete product.
>
> A multi-product customer should feel the products became more valuable because they are connected.
>
> **Standalone value is complete. Connected value compounds.**

**Recommendation:** LOCKED — Scenario B.
