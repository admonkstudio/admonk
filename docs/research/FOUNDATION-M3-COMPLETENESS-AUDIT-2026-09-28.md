## 1. Audit purpose

FOUNDATION-M3 was intended to answer the product-family questions left open after M2 locked the shared technical/platform architecture.

This audit asks:

1. Do M3-01 through M3-13 cover the intended product-family decision surface?
2. Do the decisions contradict one another?
3. Do they contradict M2?
4. Are any apparent gaps actually implementation/GTM questions that should remain deferred?
5. Is there enough clarity to promote the M3 decisions into one Product Master Plan without inventing new architecture?

The audit does **not** reopen accepted owner decisions unless a direct contradiction makes them impossible to hold together.

---

## 2. Executive finding

### Result: PASS — M3 is structurally complete enough to form the Product Master Plan.

The 13 locked decisions form a coherent product-family model.

No material architectural contradiction was found.

The remaining issues fall into three non-blocking categories:

1. **terminology normalization** from older M2/discovery language;
2. **temporary naming** that must not be mistaken for permanent brand decisions;
3. **deliberately deferred evidence questions** such as exact pricing, final names, SLA/support plans, product-specific tiers, retention durations and implementation detail.

These should be recorded as deferred work, not used to keep M3 open.
