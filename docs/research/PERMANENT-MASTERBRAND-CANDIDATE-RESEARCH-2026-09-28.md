# Permanent Masterbrand Candidate Research

**Date:** 2026-09-28  
**Status:** ACTIVE RESEARCH  
**Purpose:** Find a permanent family/masterbrand to replace the temporary working name “Admonk”.  
**Benchmark candidate:** **SIA**

---

## 1. Research rule

The owner has locked **SIA** as the first potential candidate.

SIA is **not** the final brand selection and no rebrand/migration is authorized yet.

All alternatives must compete with SIA on the same standard:

- short, ideally 3–5 letters;
- one or two easy syllables where possible;
- effortless pronunciation in English and Arabic;
- authentic ancient-language/cultural root;
- meaning connected to understanding, perception, wisdom, judgment, order or effective action;
- visually memorable;
- credible as a global technology-company masterbrand;
- not dependent on the word “AI”;
- able to sit naturally above specialist products and Jarvis;
- commercially defensible enough to justify deeper trademark/domain clearance.

Research principle:

> **Do not replace SIA with a more available but weaker name. Find an equal/stronger candidate or accept that SIA deserves deeper clearance.**

---

# 2. Candidate #1 — SIA

**Origin:** Ancient Egyptian  
**Core meaning territory:** perception, discernment, insight, intellect / thoughtful understanding  
**Pronunciation:** SEE-ah  
**Status:** **LEADING POTENTIAL CANDIDATE**

Strengths:
- 3 letters;
- easy to say and remember;
- natural in Arabic and English;
- unusually close to the role of intelligent perception/context understanding;
- authentic Egyptian story;
- elegant global technology name;
- product-family neutral;
- strong fit with the wider Sia / Hu / Heka conceptual sequence:
  - Sia — perceive / understand;
  - Hu — authoritative utterance;
  - Heka — effective power/action.

Risks:
- current AI/software/technology uses exist;
- short-name trademark/domain availability will be difficult;
- formal jurisdiction/class clearance required before final selection.

Decision:
**Keep as benchmark #1.**

# 3. Screened alternatives — Wave 1

## REKH

**Origin:** Ancient Egyptian `rḫ`  
**Meaning:** to know, learn, knowledge; can denote a knowledgeable/wise person/scholar.  
**Pronunciation:** approximately REKH / REK.

Why it was interesting:
- directly means knowing/knowledge;
- ancient Egyptian;
- four letters;
- visually distinctive;
- intellectually relevant.

Why it does not currently beat SIA:
- final consonant is less effortless internationally;
- spelling/pronunciation mismatch risk;
- an active software studio already uses REKH for product engineering, AI/agents and platforms;
- an AI-enabled browser also uses REKH.

Status: **SCREENED OUT AS PRIMARY CANDIDATE — retain as research reference.**

---

## AKH

**Origin:** Ancient Egyptian `ꜣḫ`  
**Meaning territory:** effective, efficacious, transfigured/effective being.

Why it was interesting:
- “effectiveness” maps well to the move from intelligence to outcome;
- three letters;
- authentically Egyptian.

Why it does not currently beat SIA:
- pronunciation is less effortless for global English users;
- strong funerary/afterlife association can dominate the original effectiveness idea;
- requires substantial explanation;
- weaker immediate perception/intelligence connection than SIA.

Status: **SCREENED OUT AS PRIMARY CANDIDATE.**

---

## HU

**Origin:** Ancient Egyptian  
**Meaning territory:** authoritative / effective utterance; the word that gives expression/effect to thought.

Why it was interesting:
- excellent connection to action after understanding;
- paired historically/conceptually with Sia;
- extremely short.

Why it does not currently beat SIA:
- too generic/short for search and ownership;
- English “who” phonetic collision;
- weak standalone discoverability;
- difficult trademark/domain defensibility.

Status: **BRAND-STORY CONCEPT, NOT CURRENT MASTERBRAND CANDIDATE.**

---

## MAAT

**Origin:** Ancient Egyptian  
**Meaning territory:** truth, right order, balance, justice/cosmic order.

Why it was interesting:
- extraordinary fit with governance, correctness and responsible authority;
- simple pronunciation;
- authentic Egyptian identity.

Why it does not currently beat SIA:
- already used by active software/technology companies;
- current AI research/product uses exist;
- semantically emphasizes order/governance more than perception/intelligence;
- broad historical/religious associations.

Status: **SCREENED OUT AS PRIMARY CANDIDATE.**

---

## PTah / PTAH

**Origin:** Ancient Egyptian  
**Meaning territory:** creator deity; heart conceives and tongue commands creation into effect.

Why it was interesting:
- thought → command → creation is highly aligned with agentic software;
- authentic Egyptian creation/intelligence story.

Why it does not currently beat SIA:
- difficult initial `Pt-` pronunciation internationally;
- existing current AI developer/agent product named Ptah;
- more deity-specific than SIA’s abstract faculty concept.

Status: **SCREENED OUT.**

# 5. Current research conclusion

After the first serious cross-cultural screening, **no alternative has yet matched SIA across meaning + memorability + pronunciation + cultural authenticity + masterbrand quality.**

This is useful evidence.

It suggests the final decision should not be:

> “SIA has collisions, therefore pick another ancient word.”

Instead:

> “Find a genuinely equal alternative. If none emerges, quantify the legal/commercial cost of owning SIA and decide whether that cost is worth paying.”

- company-name collision search;
- preliminary domain landscape;
- pronunciation in Arabic/English;
- unwanted modern meanings/slang;
- product-family test;
- Jarvis pairing test;
- later formal trademark clearance.

No candidate should be promoted merely because it is available.
