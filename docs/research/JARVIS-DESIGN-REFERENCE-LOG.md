## REF-001 — Textura Agency Instagram Reel

**Date added:** 2026-09-27  
**Source:** https://www.instagram.com/reel/Ddw1dLUugTJ/  
**Owner relevance:** described as very close to the intended Jarvis direction for colors, animations and interaction.

### Access limitation

The public Instagram page and cover frame were accessible, but the reel itself would not play without an authenticated Instagram session. Motion/pacing/easing therefore remain **unverified** for this reference. Do not describe them as directly observed.

### Directly observed

- dark atmospheric green → near-black background;
- restrained neon/emissive green accent around the focal screen/object;
- high-contrast white sans-serif typography;
- secondary bright yellow accent;
- low-density composition with substantial negative/dark space;
- focal object centered inside a dark environment rather than conventional dashboard framing;
- visible cursor/pointer reinforces interaction as part of the composition;
- overall impression is premium, cinematic and restrained rather than colorful SaaS-card density.

### Source-described behavior (caption, not visually verified)

The creator explicitly describes the design as:
- responding to cursor movement;
- shifting scenes with scroll;
- making the page feel aware of the visitor;
- using 3D scenes/interactions that react to movement;
- treating participation as the experience rather than presenting a passive page.

### Transferable Jarvis hypotheses

**H1 — Environment reacts to presence/intent**  
Jarvis should feel responsive to the user's input before the AI result arrives. Pointer, focus, voice/activity and task-state changes may influence the environment where meaningful.

**H2 — Motion should communicate semantic focus**  
Scene/focus movement can reinforce the earlier Interaction Graph hypothesis: whole environment → domain → capability/source → active task/evidence, rather than decorative ambient animation.

**H3 — Dark spatial canvas + selective emission**  
A dark/near-black spatial foundation with restrained luminous accents is a strong candidate direction for the Jarvis immersive surface. Exact hue/palette is not locked.

**H4 — Low visual density at the Jarvis entry surface**  
The initial Jarvis experience may benefit from large negative space and one clear focus object/state, allowing information density to emerge only when the task/workspace requires it.

**H5 — User participation over passive presentation**  
Interaction should alter meaningful product state/view/focus wherever useful. Hover/cursor/scroll reactions should reinforce orientation or affordance, not exist merely as visual spectacle.

### Connection to locked architecture

- RQ-02: immediate visible acknowledgment;
- RQ-05: conversation remains control channel while the work surface changes;
- RQ-06: dynamic workspace uses approved primitives;
- Interaction Graph hypothesis: semantic zoom into connected domains/capabilities/sources/tasks;
- RQ-11: operating lens may change which parts of the environment become prominent;
- RQ-15: degraded/waiting/active paths can be expressed spatially without implying whole-system failure.

### Do not lock from this reference alone

- continuous cursor-following animation;
- exact camera movement/easing;
- glassmorphism;
- final orb geometry;
