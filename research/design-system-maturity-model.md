# Design-System Maturity Model — Research Candidate

**Status:** Provisional research model, not approved standard.

The model is intended to help products adopt only the level of design-system maturity they actually need.

## Level 0 — Local product design
- no shared foundation required;
- local styles/components;
- intentional product-specific choices.

## Level 1 — Shared principles
- documented UX/accessibility/foundation principles;
- clear product-brand boundary;
- design review and complete-state expectations.

## Level 2 — Semantic foundation
- semantic tokens where useful;
- a small set of proven reusable components;
- documented component contracts/states;
- accessibility/responsive evidence.

## Level 3 — Domain system
- stable domain patterns;
- visual/regression validation where justified;
- component/pattern documentation;
- governed contribution/deprecation.

## Level 4 — Cross-product governed system
- proven cross-product reusable components/patterns;
- versioning/compatibility;
- ownership;
- drift management;
- consumer-impact testing.

## Research questions

- Are these levels meaningful across Admonk product types?
- Which level is sufficient for Marketing Hub initially?
- What evidence should trigger progression?
- Which capabilities should never be required solely to achieve a higher maturity label?
- Can a product intentionally remain at a lower level indefinitely?

The maturity model must not become a maturity race.
