# Product Supervisor Doctrine Research Charter

**Owner:** Admonk Studio  
**Status:** Active  
**Started:** 2026-09-26

## Primary question

What should Admonk's Product Supervisor actually believe and require in order to help humans and AI build premium, scalable, budget-conscious software products without collapsing into generic best-practice output?

## Why it matters

The existing Product Supervisor defines a strong process, but process alone does not determine:
- what quality means;
- which standards are universal;
- which choices should remain product-specific;
- what evidence is sufficient;
- how much autonomy agents should have;
- when complexity/scaling is justified.

If those answers are populated from generic AI defaults, the system risks producing generic products and fashionable architecture rather than differentiated, evidence-based decisions.

## Documents this research may influence

- `docs/PRODUCT-SUPERVISOR.md`
- `AGENTS.md`
- future `doctrine/*`
- future `standards/*`
- future `playbooks/*`
- specialist skill contracts
- project-governance templates

## Must distinguish

- universal safety/quality/interoperability requirements;
- risk-based requirements;
- maturity/stage-dependent requirements;
- product-specific strategic/design choices;
- platform-specific technical choices;
- temporary recommendations vs durable doctrine.

## What counts as evidence

Use the source hierarchy in `research/README.md`.

A source is relevant only when its scope, date/version, context and limitations are understood.

## Outside scope for this phase

- filling Marketing Hub product templates;
- selecting Marketing Hub runtime architecture;
- creating generic product/UX answers for future projects;
- adopting an engineering practice solely because it is popular;
- rewriting existing Admonk brand/design language without a research question that requires it.

## Handling disagreement

When credible sources disagree:
1. record both positions;
2. identify context/assumptions;
3. avoid false reconciliation;
4. record the unresolved disagreement;
5. make an explicit Admonk decision only when evidence and product philosophy justify it.

## Success condition

The phase succeeds when approved doctrine is:
- research-backed;
- differentiated where differentiation matters;
- explicit about limits/exceptions;
- usable by multiple AI models;
- convertible into measurable standards and practical playbooks;
- validated against BriefFlow and Marketing Hub.
