# Architecture Impact — Operating Cost

Track candidate tool/technology costs before adoption.

For each candidate:
- free/trial assumptions;
- current expected monthly cost;
- variable usage drivers;
- likely cost at first production target;
- likely cost at next scale trigger;
- implementation/maintenance time;
- support/incident burden;
- cancellation/exit cost;
- budget alert/owner;
- decision.

Do not compare license price alone; include operational complexity and maintenance time.
