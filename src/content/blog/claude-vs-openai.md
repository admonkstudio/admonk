Clients often ask us which AI is better, Claude or ChatGPT. The honest answer is that both are excellent. Both companies release new models every few months, so whichever leads on a benchmark today may not lead next quarter. A better question is which one fits the job you want done, and how to avoid being locked into either.

## The short answer

- **Claude** (by Anthropic) stands out for careful writing, long documents, and agents that carry out multi-step work reliably.
- **OpenAI** (ChatGPT and the OpenAI API) stands out for its broad feature set, including image and voice generation, and the widest range of third-party tools built on it.
- **For most businesses, the model matters less than the system around it:** your data, your rules and your workflows.

## Where each one shines

| Task | Claude | OpenAI |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Long documents (contracts, reports, policies) | Very strong: large context, careful reading | Strong |
| Writing in your brand voice | Very strong: natural, follows style guides closely | Strong |
| AI agents using your tools and data | Very strong | Very strong |
| Image generation | Not its focus | Built in |
| Voice conversations | Available | Very strong |
| Ecosystem of add-ons and plugins | Growing quickly | Largest |

Both offer business plans that keep your data out of model training, and both have APIs we can build on.

## For your team: the chat apps

If you want an assistant your team uses every day for drafting, research and analysis, try both on your real work for a week. Give them the same tasks: summarise this report, draft this proposal, analyse this spreadsheet. Most teams find a clear favourite quickly, and it isn't always the same one.

## For your business: AI built into your workflows

This is where most of the value is, and where the choice matters less than you'd think. When we build an [AI agent](/services/ai-agents) or [AI chatbot](/services/ai-chatbot), we:

1. **Ground it in your knowledge:** your services, prices, policies and past answers, so it doesn't make things up.
2. **Connect it to your tools:** CRM, email, WhatsApp and calendars, through automations in [n8n or Make](/blog/n8n-vs-make-vs-zapier).
3. **Set rules and approvals:** what the AI may do on its own, and when a person must check first.
4. **Keep the model swappable:** the workflow calls the model through one step, so switching between Claude and OpenAI is a configuration change, not a rebuild.

That last point is the most important. Prices fall and models improve every few months. A system that can switch models lets you use whichever is best and best value at the time.

## How we choose for a client

- **Reading and writing heavy work** (proposals, document review, customer replies in your voice): we usually start with Claude.
- **Image or voice generation, or an existing OpenAI setup:** OpenAI.
- **High-volume simple tasks** (tagging enquiries, extracting fields): whichever smaller, cheaper model tests best on your real examples.

We test on your own data before we commit, because that's the only benchmark that counts.
