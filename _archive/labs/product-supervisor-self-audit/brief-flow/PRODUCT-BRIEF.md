# BriefFlow — Product Brief

## Problem

Small marketing teams often create content briefs inconsistently across chat, docs and email. Writers/designers receive incomplete context and spend time asking follow-up questions.

## Intended user/customer

Primary user: small internal marketing team member.  
Buyer/customer: team owner/manager.  
Test context: fictional, controlled internal prototype only.

## Desired outcome

Create a consistent structured content brief in minutes, while keeping a human responsible for approval.

## Value hypothesis

A guided AI draft can reduce briefing time and improve consistency without needing autonomous external actions.

## MVP

- user signs in;
- enters campaign context, audience, objective and deliverable;
- AI drafts a structured brief;
- user edits and approves;
- approved briefs are stored and searchable.

## Non-goals

- no CMS publishing;
- no ad-platform actions;
- no customer PII;
- no billing;
- no external messaging;
- no autonomous execution.

## Success

A user can create, edit, approve and retrieve a usable brief without leaving the app.

## Constraints

Low-cost prototype, managed services, minimal infrastructure, no production business dependency.
