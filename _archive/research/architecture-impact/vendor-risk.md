# Architecture Impact — Vendor Risk

For every material vendor/tool candidate assess:
- dependency criticality;
- data residency/access;
- lock-in surface;
- pricing/usage sensitivity;
- API/export availability;
- rate limits;
- outage impact;
- security/compliance posture relevant to the use case;
- maintenance/change risk;
- replacement path.

Do not treat all vendor dependence as bad. Evaluate whether the managed-service value exceeds the dependency risk.
