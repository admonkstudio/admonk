# Product Supervisor Doctrine Research

**Status:** Active research workspace  
**Started:** 2026-09-26  
**Stable Supervisor release remains:** v1.1.0  
**Structure baseline:** `docs/PRODUCT-SUPERVISOR-STRUCTURE-BASELINE.md`

## Purpose

Research and deliberately author the foundational doctrine that should govern Admonk software/product delivery.

This workspace is **not authoritative doctrine**.

Research notes, AI suggestions, excerpts, competing viewpoints and draft principles remain non-canonical until an accountable human approves them and they are promoted into `doctrine/`, `standards/`, or `playbooks/`.

## Core rule

> **Standardize the process and evidence. Do not standardize the answer unless the answer is a safety, quality, or interoperability requirement.**

## Content types

### Foundational doctrine
What Admonk believes and why.

### Standards
What must be true or how quality is judged.

### Playbooks
How people/agents perform the work.

### Templates
Document shapes only.

### Skills
Specialized execution/review behavior.

### Project governance
Project-specific lifecycle truth, evidence, risks, decisions and exceptions.

### Capability and tooling discovery
What disciplines/feedback loops are needed and whether a tool/technology/connector is justified.

Research areas:
- `capability-map/`
- `tool-evaluation/`
- `architecture-impact/`
- `feedback-loops.md`
- `PARALLEL-TRACKS.md`

## Research source hierarchy

Prefer:
1. standards bodies and official specifications;
2. official framework/platform documentation;
3. established engineering/product/design books and recognized research;
4. expert organizations and respected practitioners;
5. case studies and incident/postmortem evidence;
6. industry articles and community discussions;
7. AI-generated suggestions.

AI-generated suggestions may generate research questions. They are not evidence.

## Promotion rule

Research can be promoted only when:
- sources are recorded;
- competing viewpoints are represented where material;
- applicability/limits are explicit;
- the Admonk decision is stated;
- an accountable human approves it;
- review triggers are defined.
